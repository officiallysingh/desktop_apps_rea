# Cash vs accrual, adjustments and basic principles

## In one paragraph
Under **accrual** accounting revenue is recognised when earned and expenses when incurred,
regardless of when cash moves; under **cash** basis, only when cash moves. GAAP (and Indian
companies law) require accrual. Accrual creates timing accounts: prepaid expenses (asset) and
unearned revenue (liability).

## Concepts and rules
- Accrual is required by GAAP; goal: recognise revenue/expense in the period the service is
  provided. [AMS ch.9]
- Prepaid expense: paid now for future periods → asset, expensed period by period. [AMS ch.9]
- Unearned revenue: received now for future service → liability, recognised as earned. [AMS ch.9]
- Matching principle: expenses reported in the same period as the revenue they help produce. [AMS ch.10]
- Historical cost: assets carried at original cost, not market value (objective). [AMS ch.10]
- Materiality: immaterial items may be simplified (e.g. expensing a cheap tool). [AMS ch.10, ch.11]
- Monetary unit assumption: currency treated as stable. [AMS ch.10]
- Entity concept: the business is separate from its owners (owner's personal spending is
  drawings, not expense). [AMS ch.10]
- Adjusting entries record end-of-period realities not yet in the books; typical monthly set:
  depreciation, expiry of prepaid insurance, accrued interest, bad-debt allowance. [ANA ch.6]
- [IN] Companies Act requires accrual basis for companies; many small proprietors keep
  cash-basis books for income-tax purposes. Verify against official text.

## Journal entries (Dr / Cr)
| Event | Debit | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pay 12 months' rent in advance | Prepaid rent | Bank |
| Each month | Rent expense | Prepaid rent |
| Customer pays advance for service | Bank | Unearned revenue (Advance from customer) |
| Service delivered | Unearned revenue | Revenue |
| Expense incurred, not yet paid (accrual) | Expense | Outstanding expenses (liability) |
| Accrue a month's loan interest | Interest expense | Interest payable |
| Estimate uncollectable receivables | Bad debts expense | Allowance for doubtful debts (contra-asset) |

## What this means for our product
- Company setting: accounting basis (accrual default; cash basis for small users) — affects
  reports, not the ledger.
- Recurring/scheduled journal feature for prepaid amortisation and accruals.
- Customer advances must be tracked as liabilities and adjusted against later invoices
  (also a GST event in India — advances received).
- Drawings account type for proprietors (entity concept).
