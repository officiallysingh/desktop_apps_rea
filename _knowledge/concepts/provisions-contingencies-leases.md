# Provisions, contingencies, intangibles and leases

## In one paragraph
Some obligations are uncertain in timing or amount. A **provision** is recognised (expense +
liability) when there is a present obligation from a past event, an outflow is probable and it
can be estimated reliably. A **contingent liability** is only disclosed in notes; a **contingent
asset** is never recognised (disclosed only if an inflow is probable). [FADM ch.7] [IAS 37 / Ind AS 37]

## Concepts and rules
- Provision amount = best estimate to settle the obligation; created by charging P&L. [FADM ch.7]
- Contingent liability: possible obligation dependent on future events, or a present obligation
  that is not probable/not measurable; disclose unless remote. [FADM ch.7]
- Research costs are expensed; development costs are capitalised only if strict conditions are
  met; internally generated goodwill, brands, customer lists are never recognised. [FADM ch.7] [IAS 38]
- Leases (lessee): right-of-use asset + lease liability at present value of payments; liability
  grows by interest and falls by payments. [FADM ch.7] [IFRS 16 / Ind AS 116]
- [IN] Ind AS applies to listed and larger companies; most SMB users follow Indian AS (AS 29 for
  provisions; AS 19 operating/finance lease model). Verify applicability thresholds.

## Journal entries (Dr / Cr)
| Event | Debit | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create provision (e.g. warranty) | Expense | Provision (liability) |
| Settle provision | Provision | Bank |
| Lease start (Ind AS 116) | Right-of-use asset | Lease liability |
| Lease payment | Lease liability; Interest expense | Bank |
