# Budgeting

## In one paragraph
A budget is a quantified plan for a future period used to plan and then control (compare
actual with plan). The **master budget** links a sales budget, expense and capital budgets, a
cash budget, and budgeted statements (income statement, balance sheet, cash flows). [ANA ch.10]

## Concepts and rules
- Benefits: formalises planning, communicates targets, becomes the benchmark for control. [ANA ch.10]
- Order: long-range sales forecast → sales budget → expense budgets → capital budget →
  cash budget → budgeted income statement, balance sheet and cash flows. [ANA ch.10]
- The cash budget reveals when the business will run short of cash or need to borrow. [ANA ch.10]

## What this means for our product
- Budget per ledger/group per month; budget-vs-actual report with variance and %.
- Cash forecast from receivable/payable due dates + recurring entries (very useful for SMBs).
- Capital purchase plan linked to the fixed asset register.
